`apps/web/public/hsk30-index.json` and `apps/web/public/hsk30-extra.json` are derived from the HSK 3.0 word list in
[ivankra/hsk30](https://github.com/ivankra/hsk30). That data is itself largely derived from
[elkmovie/hsk30](https://github.com/elkmovie/hsk30) and
[shawkynasr/HSK-official-Query-System](https://github.com/shawkynasr/HSK-official-Query-System),
all MIT licensed. The original word list is a work of the Ministry of Education of the People's
Republic of China.

`apps/web/public/grammar-catalog.json` is derived from the HSK 3.0 grammar list (2021 edition) in
[ivankra/hsk30](https://github.com/ivankra/hsk30), also a work of the Ministry of Education of the
People's Republic of China. Only the point names and formal descriptions are kept; the raw CSV stays out of git.
